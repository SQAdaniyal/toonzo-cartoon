# ToonZo Official — Project Plan

## 1. Brand Objective
ToonZo Official is a zero-budget-first cartoon production and publishing platform designed to build an original cartoon identity, create repeated content at scale, and test monetization opportunities through YouTube and related channels.

## 2. Core Workflow
Idea -> Script -> Characters -> Storyboard -> Voiceover -> Footage -> Render -> QA -> Publish -> Analytics -> Optimize -> Monetize

## 3. Target Production Model
- 1 long-form cartoon episode per day
- 2–4 Shorts per day depending on quality and capacity
- Focus on family-friendly, kid-safe storytelling
- Use free tools and local processing wherever possible

## 4. Production Phases
1. Channel strategy and content planning
2. Character and story development
3. Visual asset generation and scene creation
4. Voiceover and audio production
5. Rendering and assembly
6. Quality assurance and compliance
7. Metadata, SEO and publishing preparation
8. YouTube upload and distribution
9. Analytics and retention review
10. Optimization and monetization readiness

## 5. Success Metrics
- Publish consistency
- View retention and click-through rate
- Character recognizability
- Repeatable production without paid dependency
- Weekly quality improvement based on analytics

## 6. Operating Principles
- Prefer free or local tools first
- Maintain a clear content pipeline
- Keep channels and credentials separated by platform
- Validate outputs before publishing
- Avoid automated engagement manipulation
- Require human review for uncertain copyright, safety, or monetization issues
